# Synchroniser simulation, n = 100 unless stated, seeds [1, 2, 3]

Latencies in ms, pooled over seeds. *deliver*: block built to admitted at another validator. *decide*: leader block built to decided (committed directly, or decided by a later direct commit). *direct*: fraction of leaders committed directly. *extra*: symbol and pull bytes per validator per round, and as a share of push bytes. *pulls*, *decoded*, *dup*: per validator per round (requests, fragments decoded, duplicate fragments). *catch-up*: from the end of the outage to the validator's first block at or above the median round of the others.
